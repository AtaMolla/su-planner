How to use SU Planner

Plan your whole degree in a few steps. Everything you choose is saved in this browser.

  1. 1Pick your program
  2. 24-year plan
  3. 3Semester times
  4. 4Weekly schedule
  5. 5Prerequisites
1

Pick your program

Do this once, in the side panel. Every page uses it.

  1. Choose your program.
  2. Choose the year you started. Your requirements depend on it.
  3. Optional: add a double major or minors.
2

Plan your 4 years

Open 4-year plan →

Your eight semesters, filled in for you.

  1. Suggest plan fills every semester. Press it any time to start over.
  2. The bars show what each requirement still needs.
  3. Drag a course to another semester. Add courses with + in the course list.
  4. Dashed cards are electives you still choose: click one, then pick a course.

Red text on a course means it comes before its prerequisite. Move it to a later semester.

3

See every possible timetable

Open Semester times →

For each semester of your plan, the page finds every choice of sections with no clashes. Flip through them and keep the one you like.

  1. Pick a semester from your plan.
  2. Use ‹ › (or the ← → keys) to see each timetable. “Timetable 2 of 80” means 80 different weeks work.
  3. Like one? Keep this one saves its sections.
  4. Or choose a section yourself. Only timetables with it are shown; Auto frees it again.

A red warning means two courses clash in every timetable. Move one of them to another semester in your 4-year plan.

4

Build this term's schedule

Open Weekly schedule →

Pick sections by hand for the current term.

  1. Search a course by code, name or instructor.
  2. Open a course and tick one section of each part (lecture, recitation…).
  3. Copy CRNs when you are ready to register.
5

Check prerequisites

Open Prerequisites →

See which courses open which.

  1. Click a course. Arrows show what it needs and what it opens.
  2. The side panel lists them.

Good to know

  • Your plan is saved in this browser only. Another browser or your phone starts empty.
  • Class times come from SU's latest Fall and Spring schedules. Future terms may change.
  • Check your final plan with your advisor and SU's Degree Evaluation.
