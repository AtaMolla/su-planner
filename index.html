How it works

  1. Pick your program above: the list then follows its degree requirements, required courses first.
  2. Find a course by code, name or instructor.
  3. Open it and tick one section from each group. It lands on your timetable once every group has a pick.
  4. Hover a section to preview it: free, clash.
  5. Click a block on the timetable to change that course. × removes a section.
  6. Press Copy CRNs when you're ready to register.
